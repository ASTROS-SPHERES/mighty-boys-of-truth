# Website Interaction Approval Checklist

The former seven-stat Hero Matchup simulator has been retired because it no longer represents the developed product. `hero-matchup-demo.html` now directs visitors to the current flagship experience without presenting superseded rules or data.

## Mighty 365 draw

The device-local Mighty 365 sample draw remains active with the two approved supplied artworks:

- Family & Respect — morning sample
- Wisdom & Choices — evening sample

Future additions still require approved artwork and accessible text.

## Mighty Hero Matchup

The public site now presents the approved developed product:

- two players;
- ages 8+;
- 20–30 minutes;
- Faith, Courage, Wisdom, Leadership and Obedience;
- Draw, Swap, Shield, Focus, Rally and Strike Command Tokens;
- approved game components, campaign posters and flagship video QR card.

No legacy seven-stat simulator is presented as current gameplay.

## Mighty Bible Battle

A separate interactive board-game simulation still requires approved production rules, questions, answers and exception handling before it can resolve valid turns.
