# Production Workflow Audit

Reviewed 30 August 2026 against `Mighty_Boys_of_Truth_WebRevamp_Workflow.docx`.

The 27-page workflow rendered cleanly with no clipping, overlap, tracked changes or comments. Its execution standards remain useful, but its product assumptions are superseded by later owner decisions:

- there are four flagship products, not three;
- product 02 is Mighty Bible Discovery, not Mighty Bible 365;
- Mighty Bible Discovery is now a 120-card map-integrated system;
- Mighty Hero Matchup is the separate hero stat-battle product;
- all four products use the public status Introduction;
- the ages are minimum guidance only, with no upper limit.
