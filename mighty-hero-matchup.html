The experience

Bible heroes meet championship strategy.

Mighty Hero Matchup is a dedicated two-player game built around hero selection, attribute matchups, tactical Command Tokens, round victories and a final championship result.

The five attributes

Different heroes. Different strengths.

Every matchup is shaped by five memorable qualities: Faith, Courage, Wisdom, Leadership and Obedience. These are gameplay attributes designed to encourage conversation about the heroes and their stories.

How the championship unfolds

From first reveal to final victory.

A clear, energetic match flow makes every component part of the action.

  1. 01

    Prepare the Arena

    Set out the Championship Arena, Victory Racks, Champion Stand, Round Marker, Command Tokens and Victory Crests. Split the Hero Deck between the two players.

  2. 02

    Reveal the Matchup

    Bring heroes into the arena and compare the selected attribute: Faith, Courage, Wisdom, Leadership or Obedience.

  3. 03

    Command the Battle

    Use Draw, Swap, Shield, Focus, Rally and Strike at the right moment to influence the round and create comeback opportunities.

  4. 04

    Build Your Victory

    Win rounds, earn Victory Crests and fill your Victory Rack as the championship progresses.

  5. 05

    Crown the Champion

    Complete the final challenge, raise the winning hero on the Champion Stand and record the result in the Hall of Champions.
