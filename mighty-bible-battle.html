Mighty Bible Battle

The purpose

Bring the Bible challenge to the table.

Mighty Bible Battle is the family and group-play experience in the flagship family. It creates a shared setting for participation, Bible questions, movement and realm-based challenges.

The format

Designed for a group moment.

The approved concept supports two to six players, an expected 20–35 minute play time, six Bible realms and three difficulty levels.
