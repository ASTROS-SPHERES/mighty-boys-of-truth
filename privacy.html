Pre-Launch Privacy Notice

How information submitted through the Mighty Boys of Truth pre-launch signup is used.

Responsible party: All Mighty Publishing, for the Mighty Boys of Truth pre-launch campaign.

Information we collect

The signup collects the information needed to manage the pre-launch community, including first name, email address, country, supporter type and the recorded consent choice.

Why we collect it

We use submitted information to send requested Mighty Boys of Truth crowdfunding launch announcements, important pre-launch updates and closely related campaign information.

Your choice and consent

Joining the list is voluntary. The signup requires an affirmative consent choice. You may withdraw from future marketing communications at any time by following the instructions provided in campaign communications or by replying to a campaign email with an unsubscribe or deletion request.

Service providers

Form submissions are processed using Formspree as the website form service. Information may also be processed by email and mailing-list services used by All Mighty Publishing to communicate with supporters. We do not intend to sell pre-launch supporter information to advertisers.

Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably required for the pre-launch, crowdfunding campaign and related communications, or until you unsubscribe or request deletion, subject to legitimate legal and record-keeping requirements. Individual service providers may apply their own technical retention periods.

Your information rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held for the pre-launch list. You may also withdraw consent for marketing communications. The easiest contact method is to reply directly to a Mighty Boys of Truth or All Mighty Publishing campaign email.

Children

This pre-launch list is intended for adults such as parents, guardians, supporters, churches, schools and crowdfunding backers. Children should not submit personal information directly through the signup form; a parent or guardian should sign up on their behalf.

This is a practical privacy notice for the current pre-launch signup and may be updated as the campaign systems and service providers develop.
