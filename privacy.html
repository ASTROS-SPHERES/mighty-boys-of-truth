Website Privacy Notice

A plain-language explanation of adult forms, child-safety boundaries, device-local progress and privacy-aware website events.

Responsible party: All Mighty Publishing, for Mighty Boys of Truth. Contact Mightyboysoftruth1@outlook.com.

Information adults submit

The adult launch-list form asks for first name, last name, email address, country or region, relationship to the mission, and confirmation that the person submitting the form is at least 18. The adult enquiry form asks for an adult name, email address, optional organisation, enquiry type, message and adult confirmation.

Why we collect it

We use submitted information to manage the launch community, reply to adult enquiries and send requested product introductions, approved previews, crowdfunding announcements, release news and closely related updates.

Mighty Vault

Mighty Vault stores the digital Hero collection, Vault Points, daily streak, last daily-pack date and any uncollected open pack in local browser storage on the device. The Vault does not require a child account, name, age, school, location, photo, message or payment. The website does not send the local Vault collection through the adult forms. Clearing browser data or moving to a different device can reset the collection.

Mission Passport and daily sample

The Mission Passport and daily sample choice use local browser storage. The stored values are non-personal progress marks, the current date and a sample index. They remain on the device, are not sent through the website forms and can be reset from the Mission Passport.

Privacy-aware website events

The website emits simple event names such as product view, card reveal, hero comparison and audience-path selection. The code does not include form contents, card responses, names or child information in these events. No external analytics service is enabled in this revamp unless separately configured and reviewed.

Offline support

A service worker may cache public website pages, styles, scripts and approved images on the device so parts of the site can reopen more reliably. The cache does not include adult form submissions.

Your choice and consent

Joining or sending an enquiry is voluntary and requires an affirmative adult confirmation. You can ask to stop future messages or request deletion by replying to a campaign email or contacting us.

Service providers and embedded media

Form submissions are processed by Formspree. Information may also be handled by email or mailing-list services used by All Mighty Publishing. Embedded services such as YouTube may receive technical information when their content is loaded or played and apply their own privacy practices.

Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell adult form information to advertisers. Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the stated communication or enquiry, subject to legitimate legal, security and record-keeping needs.

Children

The launch list and enquiry form are intended for adults. Children should not submit personal information; an adult should communicate on their behalf. Mighty Vault and the public interactive experiences do not require a child profile, name, age, school, location, photo or message.
