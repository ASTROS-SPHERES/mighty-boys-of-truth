Mighty Boys of Truth

Pre-Launch Privacy Notice

A plain-language summary of how information submitted through the Mighty Boys of Truth pre-launch signup will be used.

Responsible party: All Mighty Publishing, for the Mighty Boys of Truth pre-launch campaign.

Information we plan to collect

The pre-launch signup is intended to collect only the information needed to keep supporters informed, such as your first name, email address and the type of supporter you identify as (for example parent, family, church, school or crowdfunding backer).

Why we collect it

We will use submitted information to send requested Mighty Boys of Truth crowdfunding launch announcements, important pre-launch updates and closely related campaign information.

Your choice and consent

Joining the list is voluntary. Before the live signup is activated, the form will include a clear consent statement so that people understand what they are signing up to receive. Campaign emails should also provide a simple way to unsubscribe.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by the form or email service used to operate the pre-launch list. It should not be sold to advertisers. Any service provider selected for the live form will be identified or linked through its own privacy information where appropriate.

How long information is kept

Information should be retained only while it is needed for the pre-launch and related Mighty Boys of Truth communications, or until a person unsubscribes or asks for deletion, subject to any legitimate legal or record-keeping requirements.

Your information rights

You may ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held for the pre-launch list, and you may withdraw from marketing communications. A dedicated contact method will be added to this notice when the live signup service is connected.

Children

This pre-launch list is intended for adults such as parents, guardians, supporters, churches, schools and crowdfunding backers. Children should not submit personal information directly through the signup form.

This notice is a practical pre-launch privacy summary and is not a substitute for legal advice. It will be finalized with the live form provider and contact details before real supporter data is collected.
