Clear answers

Frequently asked questions.

Only confirmed introduction-stage information is published here. Product rules, campaign claims and release details are added after approval.

Are these age ranges restrictions?

No. Ages 7+ for Mighty 365 and ages 8+ for the other three products are minimum-age guidance only. None of the four products has an upper age limit.

What does “Introduction” mean?

Introduction is the current public status of all four products. The website presents approved product visuals and confirmed facts without making unverified manufacturing, price or release-date claims.

How are Mighty Bible Discovery and Mighty Hero Matchup different?

Mighty Bible Discovery is a 120-card, map-integrated journey across heroes, places, events and truth. Mighty Hero Matchup is a separate 60-hero stat-battle card game using seven approved matchup statistics.

Why are the books not shown?

The books and the earlier collector-box story are archived from the public journey for now so the four flagship products remain clear.

Does the site collect information from children?

No child account is required. The launch list and enquiry forms are for adults, while Mission Passport progress stays only in the visitor’s browser and can be reset at any time.

Where can I ask about family, ministry, education or retail use?

Use the appropriate pathway above or visit the adult enquiry page. We publish only verified product information and will add fuller packs when approved.
