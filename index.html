Our mission

Boys need something worth standing for.

Mighty Boys of Truth gives boys exciting stories and experiences that strengthen faith, shape character, encourage courage and make truth something they are proud to live by.

It is adventurous and aspirational while remaining grounded in Christian faith and practical everyday choices.

Meet Eli

The boy who guides readers into the adventure.

Eli is curious, courageous, faith-filled and adventurous. He is learning, growing and discovering what it means to trust God when life is exciting, uncertain or difficult.
