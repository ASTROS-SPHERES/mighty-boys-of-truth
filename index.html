Why this exists

Boys need something worth standing for.

Mighty Boys of Truth is built around a simple mission: give boys exciting stories and experiences that strengthen faith, shape character, encourage courage and make truth something they are proud to live by.

It is designed to feel adventurous and aspirational while remaining grounded in Christian faith and practical everyday choices.

Meet Eli

The boy who guides readers into the adventure.

Eli is curious, courageous, faith-filled and adventurous. He is learning, growing, asking questions and discovering what it means to trust God when life is exciting, uncertain or difficult.

Rather than being presented as perfect, Eli gives boys someone relatable to travel with — from the stormy world of Eli and the Unshakeable Anchor into the broader Mighty Boys of Truth universe.
