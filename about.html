Our mission

Truth boys can discover, practise and share.

Mighty Boys of Truth is a growing family of Christian tools created to help boys engage with Scripture, strengthen character and build meaningful faith-filled moments with the adults who guide them.

What the mission makes possible

From Biblical truth to everyday life.

The product family supports three connected kinds of engagement without making every product do the same job.

01

Discover

Make the people, places, events, journeys and truths of Scripture easier to explore and remember.

02

Engage

Use daily missions, map exploration, hero matchups and shared challenges to turn attention into participation.

03

Grow

Create more opportunities for character-building conversations between boys and the adults raising them.
