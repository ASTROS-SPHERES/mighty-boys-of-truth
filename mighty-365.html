The purpose

Make truth part of the day.

Mighty 365 is designed for consistent daily character and discipleship formation. Each day begins with an age-appropriate Biblical truth and a practical mission that can be carried into real life.

Adult guidance is encouraged so the daily draw can become a meaningful conversation rather than a task completed alone.

The rhythm

Morning action. Evening reflection.

The morning side introduces the theme, God’s Truth, Mighty Truth and Today’s Mission. The evening side creates space to debrief, pray, consider a Mighty Move and mark the mission complete.

Minimum-age guidance: Recommended from age 7, with no upper age limit.
